## F. Accepted high-level design boundary

This summary identifies the accepted boundary; the exact design remains the
source of detailed requirements.

- **PKE2RF02 / PKE2WB02:** qualified physical representation and codec/profile
  primitives preserve unchanged logical canonical evidence, `records.encode()`
  bytes, semantic digests and required evidence content.
- **Physical durable archive authority:** actual durable physical bytes govern
  archive accounting. The independent logical workload floor qualifies
  postprocessing workload; it supplies no archive credit. Physical and logical
  floors are independently measured and satisfied, with **no physical/logical
  cross-credit** or substitution.
- **Two-component P/K/D/O constructor:** repetitive text supplies logical volume
  and deterministic entropy controls physical size. Actual canonical bodies and
  complete frames determine sizes. **Artificial Z is forbidden.**
- **Truthful 30x16 reference constructor:** retain the complete 479-entry catalog,
  truthful semantic and K/D copied-reference bindings, corrected copy-aware
  bounds, and the three K accounting slots. Artificial padding must not replace
  required truthful evidence.
- **Text-family +4096 overshoot window:** positive-floor P, K, O-primary, O-repeat
  and D require `T_P <= P_actual <= T_P + 4096` together with `L_actual >= T_L`
  and every applicable semantic/composite cap. This is a procedural overshoot
  guard, not a new archive allowance. Zero-row Oracle stays exactly zero and is
  exempt from the window.
- **Reference exception with bounded minimum eligible selection:** reference R
  has no +4096 window. Within at most eight unique candidate evaluations, select
  the eligible lexicographic minimum `(actual_physical_bytes,
  actual_logical_bytes, q)`, not merely the first successful candidate. All
  reference floors, truthful bindings, caps and global budget still apply.
- **Global `B_proc` procedural-excess guard:** reserve the full fixed allowance
  and subtract accepted physical minima from the archive cap. Independently
  validate and reserve the complete unit's actual excess before its first
  attributable write; record committed excess only after required durability.
  No family shortfall may offset another's excess; K/D copied-reference bytes
  must not be charged again to R. Rejected trials and unused window/allowance
  provide no credit. Final ledger reconciliation and independent fixed/archive
  checks remain mandatory.
- **V1/V2 isolation:** preserve historical V1 evidence and decoding; reject
  incompatible schema/profile/basis mixing. Do not supplement or migrate old
  evidence with new V2 facts.
- **Independent worker/parent/coordinator/readback validation:** each boundary
  independently checks its required facts. Reuse is confined to the accepted
  private context within one boundary; append-only immutable keys, durability
  before progress, partial-failure poison and independent final readback remain.
- **Mandatory bounded large-reference performance diagnostic before large
  qualification:** use the truthful 30x16/479-entry reference with the accepted
  `q=463,000` witness, actual K/D copies/bindings and implemented qualified

  qualification. This is implementation qualification evidence, not R0
